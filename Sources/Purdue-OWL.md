## Description

Purdue OWL provides free writing and instructional materials.

The SDGKU library recommends it for assistance with APA citation
and formatting.

## Best Uses

Use Purdue OWL for:

- APA style guidance
- Paper formatting
- In-text citations
- Reference-list formatting
- Academic writing guidance

## AI Instructions

When checking APA formatting:

1. Use Purdue OWL as guidance.
2. Preserve the actual bibliographic facts from the original source.
3. Do not invent missing citation information simply to complete a citation.
4. Flag missing information when necessary.
