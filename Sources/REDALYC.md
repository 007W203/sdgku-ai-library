# REDALYC

## Full Name

Red de Revistas Cientificas de America Latina y el Caribe,
Espana y Portugal

## Access

http://www.redalyc.org/coleccionHome.oa

## Description

REDALYC provides access to scientific and scholarly journals with a strong
focus on Latin America, Spain and Portugal.

The SDGKU library information identifies it as useful for Spanish-speaking
students and notes coverage of hundreds of scientific peer-reviewed journals
from 22 Latin American countries.

## Best Uses

Use REDALYC for:

- Spanish-language academic research
- Latin American research
- Peer-reviewed journal articles
- Regional perspectives
- Social sciences
- Business
- Economics
- Education
- International and cross-cultural research

## AI Instructions

- Preserve the original language of article titles.
- Translate titles only when useful, and label translations clearly.
- Prefer peer-reviewed material.
- Do not assume English-language summaries accurately represent the full article.
- Record authors, journal, date and stable URL when available.
