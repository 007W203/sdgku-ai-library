Prefer focused academic searches.

Examples:

    "global business" AND sustainability

    entrepreneurship AND "Latin America"

    "foreign direct investment" AND Mexico

Use filters such as:

- Peer Reviewed
- Full Text
- Publication Date
- Subject
- Academic Journals

---

# AI Instructions

When using EBSCO:

- Prefer peer-reviewed material when appropriate.
- Do not invent database results.
- Do not claim an article was read unless its content was retrieved.
- Preserve article titles and author names accurately.
- Record DOI or stable URLs whenever available.
- Save useful downloaded research in `..\Research\Articles`.
- Save research notes in `..\Research\Notes`.
- Save citation exports in `..\Research\Citations` or `Source-Exports`.
