# JSTOR

## Access

https://www.jstor.org/

## Description

JSTOR provides access to more than 12 million academic journal articles,
books and primary sources across approximately 75 disciplines.

## Best Uses

Use JSTOR for:

- Scholarly journal articles
- Humanities
- History
- Social sciences
- Business
- Economics
- Political science
- Primary sources
- Books and book chapters
- Literature reviews
- Historical scholarship

## Search Guidance

Search using focused subject terms and exact phrases.

Examples:

    "global entrepreneurship"

    "international business" AND culture

    globalization AND developing countries

Use publication-date and subject filters when appropriate.

## AI Instructions

- Prefer scholarly and peer-reviewed material when appropriate.
- Do not infer that full text is available merely because a search result exists.
- Record author, title, publication, date, DOI and stable URL when available.
- Distinguish historical scholarship from current data when relevant.
- Save useful materials in the SDGKU Research folders.
